From the author · 作者寄语

Ideas should have somewhere to keep moving.

Hello, I am Junhan Sun, the first author of INTACT. This is an open community for world-model research. We will share new INTACT results and ideas, and welcome reproductions, constructive criticism, collaboration, and early-stage discussion.

你好，我是 INTACT 第一作者孙俊涵。这里是一个面向世界模型研究的开放交流社区。我们会持续同步 INTACT 的实验进展与新想法，也欢迎复现、建设性批评、合作和自由讨论。

INTACT's launch received attention from Yann LeCun and members of the JEPA community. This space keeps that conversation open: research questions first, status second.

INTACT 发布后得到了 Yann LeCun 和 JEPA 社区研究者的关注。我们希望把这份讨论延续下去：以研究问题为先，以开放交流为本。
